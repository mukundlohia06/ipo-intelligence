SECTION I: SUMMARY OF THE OFFER
The Offer comprises a Public Issue of Equity Shares of face value of ₹ 10 each of ABC Industries Limited for cash at a price band of ₹ 450 to ₹ 475 per Equity Share aggregating up to ₹ 12,500.00 million (₹ 1,250.00 Crores).
The Offer comprises a Fresh Issue of up to ₹ 8,000.00 million (₹ 800.00 Crores) by the Company and an Offer for Sale (OFS) of up to ₹ 4,500.00 million (₹ 450.00 Crores) by the Selling Shareholders including the Promoter Group.
The minimum Bid lot size is 31 Equity Shares and in multiples of 31 Equity Shares thereafter.
The Equity Shares are proposed to be listed on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and BSE Limited.

SECTION II: OBJECTS OF THE OFFER
Our Company proposes to utilise the Net Proceeds from the Fresh Issue towards the following objects:
1. Setting up of a dedicated automated clean-tech heat exchanger and hydrogen-ready thermal component manufacturing facility in Sanand Phase II, Gujarat: ₹ 3,500.00 million (₹ 350.00 Crores).
2. Prepayment or repayment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding borrowings availed by our Company: ₹ 2,500.00 million (₹ 250.00 Crores).
3. General corporate purposes and issue related expenses: ₹ 2,000.00 million (₹ 200.00 Crores).

SECTION III: OUR BUSINESS
ABC Industries Limited is an engineering and manufacturing company specializing in precision heat transfer solutions, specialized alloy pressure vessels, and clean-tech components used in renewable energy, green hydrogen, and petrochemical sectors.
For Fiscal 2024, our Revenue from Operations stood at ₹ 12,505.00 million (₹ 1,250.50 Crores), representing a growth of 42.02% over Fiscal 2023. Our EBITDA margin for Fiscal 2024 was 14.41% and Profit After Tax (PAT) margin was 7.60%.

SECTION IV: RISK FACTORS
Internal Risk Factors:
1. Significant Customer Concentration:
Our top three customers accounted for 68.42% of our total revenue from operations in Fiscal 2024, with our single largest customer contributing 38.15%. We do not have long-term volume commitments with these customers. The loss of any of our top three customers, or a significant decrease in demand from them, will have an immediate adverse impact on our business, cash flows, and operational results.

2. Divergence and Negative Operating Cash Flows:
While the Company recorded a Profit After Tax of ₹ 951.00 million in Fiscal 2024, cash flow from operating activities was negative ₹ 254.00 million (-₹ 25.40 Crores). This negative cash flow was primarily driven by significant working capital requirements, specifically an expansion of trade receivables from ₹ 1,420.00 million to ₹ 3,110.00 million, and strategic inventory stockpiling of nickel alloys. If we are unable to recover receivables in a timely manner, our liquidity position may deteriorate.

3. Significant Indebtedness and Debt Service Obligations:
As of March 31, 2024, our Total Outstanding Borrowings were ₹ 3,805.00 million (₹ 380.50 Crores), resulting in a Debt-to-Equity ratio of 1.45. While we intend to repay ₹ 250.00 Crores from issue proceeds, high debt service obligations expose us to interest rate fluctuations.

4. Outstanding Legal and Tax Litigations:
The Company and its Promoters are involved in 4 pending direct tax assessment disputes before the Income Tax Appellate Tribunal (ITAT) amounting to an aggregate contingent liability of ₹ 142.00 million (₹ 14.20 Crores). An adverse verdict could require immediate payment and reduce our reported net worth.
